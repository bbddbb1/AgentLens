## Scenario Set

1. Identify run outcome, current/final phase, and major phase sequence from one selected frame.
2. Follow one activity across summary, graph, timeline, and inspector without frame drift.
3. Diagnose one failure/wait and identify whether evidence is missing, redacted, encrypted, oversized, or contradictory.
4. Reach the supporting evidence for a selected story element in at most two interactions.
5. Repeat the comprehension flow on Corpus A, Corpus B, and Corpus C.
